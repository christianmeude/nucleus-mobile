# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Enrolled students and faculty at National University — Dasmariñas.

- **Students** use it for browsing research, submitting papers, and tracking their submissions.
- **Faculty** use it as a command center to manage reviews, assignments, and workload.

## Product Purpose

The unified mobile client for students and faculty to manage, submit, and review research papers at National University Dasmariñas. It brings research management into a convenient mobile experience.

## Positioning

A dedicated, university-specific native mobile interface providing real-time tracking of research submissions and review assignments directly on users' phones, streamlining the process compared to generic web portals.

## Operating Context

On-the-go access for students tracking their submissions and receiving notifications; quick dashboard insight for faculty managing their review queues and workloads.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React Native (Expo) mobile application.
- Supabase backend (Auth, Database, Storage).
- Session persistence, offline capabilities considerations.
- Distinct views for Student Dashboard vs. Faculty Dashboard.
- Strict terminology (e.g., "Review Queue" instead of "Assignment List").

## Brand Commitments

Strict adherence to National University — Dasmariñas brand guidelines (Navy Blue, Gold, specific logo).

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing React Native app architecture with routing and Supabase integration.
- `DESIGN.md` capturing the established visual world.
- Detailed terminology rules in `CONTEXT.md`.

## Product Principles

- **Clarity of status**: Students and faculty must instantly know where a paper stands in the review process.
- **Role-focused command centers**: Dashboards surface immediate priorities, distinguishing actionable items from historical feeds.
- **Native feel**: The experience adapts to platform norms (iOS/Android) for a seamless, expected feel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accessibility requirements beyond standard best practices.
